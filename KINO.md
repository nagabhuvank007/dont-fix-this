# Kino — the presentation film

~50 seconds. Doubles as the submission video, so this is not extra work.
No shooting. Every frame is a screen recording of your own app.

## What to capture before you edit (10 min)
Record at full resolution, slowly, in this order:
1. Your real letter being typed, or sitting still on the ivory page.
2. The protected sentence being highlighted. Slow. Let the cursor hesitate.
3. The prediction tap.
4. Each polish level appearing, one after another.
5. The ghost screen. Record this twice as long as feels necessary.
6. The Pangram result arriving, with the real number.
7. The end card.

Keep the mouse out of frame where you can. Nobody should think about a cursor.

## The edit

| Time | On screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Your letter, still, on ivory. No title yet. | room tone, your voice begins reading it |
| 0:06–0:09 | Title: DON'T FIX THIS | voice continues over it |
| 0:09–0:14 | The sentence gets highlighted | voice reaches that exact sentence |
| 0:14–0:18 | "Will this sentence survive?" — the tap | one soft note |
| 0:18–0:32 | Light, then medium, then heavy. Text smoothing, tightening. | voice still reading YOUR original |
| 0:32–0:42 | The ghosts. Hold. Do not cut. | voice stops mid-sentence. room tone only |
| 0:42–0:46 | Pangram's real result | nothing |
| 0:46–0:50 | "AI can make your words better. / Only you can decide what better means." | the held note resolves |

## The one idea that makes it a film instead of a product demo

**Read your own letter aloud over the whole thing, and never re-record it.**

As the polish escalates, the words on screen stop matching the words in your mouth. Your
voice is still saying "I still open the fridge expecting your food to be there" while the
screen now reads "I deeply cherish the comforting memories of home."

The viewer hears the human and reads the machine at the same time. Nobody has to explain
the point — the gap does it.

Then, at the ghost screen, your voice stops mid-sentence. That silence is the film.

## Rules
- **One continuous audio bed, start to finish.** No audio cuts. The ear hears seams the
  eye forgives, and a seamless bed is what made the earlier test cut read as one piece.
- **Your real voice, one take, imperfect.** Do not re-record it smooth. A clean read
  undoes the whole argument. Breaths and stumbles stay in.
- **No music with a melody.** One sustained note or nothing. Melody tells people how to
  feel, and the piece is about them deciding that themselves.
- **No narration, no explainer titles, no captions.** The only words are the letter's.
- **Real numbers only.** Whatever Pangram actually returned. Never a mocked-up score.
- Hold the ghost shot longer than is comfortable. That is the whole film's payoff.

## If you are behind at 8:30
Cut to 15 seconds: letter -> highlight -> heavy polish -> ghosts -> end card.
Your voice over all of it. Still a real edit, still makes the point.
