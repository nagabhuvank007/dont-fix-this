Dear Amma,

I'll be home for the winter, and the thought of it carries me through the week. Distance has a way of clarifying things. I've learned to cook for myself, though nothing I make tastes the way your kitchen does, and I've learned that being busy is a poor excuse for being quiet. Forgive me for the calls I've rushed through. What I want most is simple: to sit beside you, to eat well, to tell you everything. Strange, the things absence makes precious — even the small corrections I once resented.

Until very soon,
Bhuvan
